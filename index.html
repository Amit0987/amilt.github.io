The story

How 300 records became a 30% improvement

Illustrative model, not client data
01

The month starts as noise

300+ records land every month. Each has a category, a priority, a turnaround time and a status. Taken one by one they look routine. Together they are noise.

02

Then comes the pattern

I validate the data with SQL, then group it by cause. A few causes account for most of the repeats. Suddenly there is something to act on.

03

The line starts to bend

I rank by impact, document each root cause and hand fixes to the owning teams. Recurring issues fall by 30%.

04

Everything on one screen

The monthly dashboard shows SLA compliance of 85–90% and turnaround trends in one view. Actions are tracked to closure in Jira.
